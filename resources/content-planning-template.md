## Review before scheduling

- [ ] The post supports the stated goal and is useful to the intended audience.
- [ ] Names, claims, dates, prices, links, and other facts are verified.
- [ ] Copy is accurate, clear, and consistent with the brand voice.
- [ ] Each channel version fits its intended format and current requirements.
- [ ] Media is owned or appropriately licensed, and accessibility details are included.
- [ ] Required disclosures or specialist approvals are complete.
- [ ] Correct account, date, time, and timezone are selected.
- [ ] A person is responsible for checking publication status.
